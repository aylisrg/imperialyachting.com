Questions.

Common questions about our fleet and charter options.

What types of yachts does Imperial Yachting offer?

Three vessels: the Monte Carlo 6, a 60ft Italian flybridge motor yacht for up to 18 guests; the Van Dutch 40, a 40ft Dutch day cruiser for up to 10 guests; and the EVO 43, a 43ft Italian transformable day yacht with XTension deck.

Are the yachts available for overnight charters?

The Van Dutch 40 and EVO 43 are primarily day charter vessels, but monthly rentals are available during all seasons. The Monte Carlo 6 is available for overnight and multi-day charters.
