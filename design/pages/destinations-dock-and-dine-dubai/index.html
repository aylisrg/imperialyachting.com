Dock
& Dine.

Free yacht mooring at 20 waterfront marinas. Since September 2025, a government-backed yacht-to-table programme by Dubai Maritime Authority.

01

What it
is.

A government-backed programme turning Dubai into the world's leading yacht-tourism hub.

  • 01Complimentary berth for up to one hour at any participating marina while you dine.
  • 02Book your mooring slot in as little as 5 minutes, with windows up to 60 minutes before arrival.
  • 03Tender boats at select marinas to transfer guests from yacht to restaurant jetty.
  • 04Uniform guidelines across all 20 marinas.
  • 05Designed for Dubai's yacht season (October–April).

Three
steps.

Three simple steps from yacht to table.

01

Contact the marina or restaurant

Call or message the marina directly or reach the restaurant to confirm a mooring slot. Booking windows range from 5 to 60 minutes.

02

Dock

Arrive at the participating marina and dock at the allocated berth. Mooring is complimentary for up to one hour. Tender boats are available at select locations.

03

Dine

Walk straight into the waterfront restaurant, enjoy your meal, and return to your yacht. DMA Coastguard line: +971 800 8855.
