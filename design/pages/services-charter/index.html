Questions.

Common questions, answered as on the live page.

What is the difference between a day charter and a weekly charter in Dubai?

A day charter typically runs 4-8 hours and is perfect for sunset cruises, celebrations, or sightseeing along Dubai's coastline. Weekly charters offer 7 consecutive days of yacht access with the flexibility to explore further destinations including Abu Dhabi and the east coast. Weekly rates offer 15-25% savings compared to daily booking.

Can I customize my yacht charter itinerary?

Absolutely. While we suggest popular routes including Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Ain Dubai, and World Islands, your captain will work with you to create a bespoke itinerary. Special stops for swimming, snorkeling, or dining at waterfront restaurants can be arranged. Corporate clients can also request specific waypoints for events.

What happens if weather conditions are bad on my charter day?

Safety is our top priority. If weather conditions make sailing unsafe, we offer free rescheduling to an alternative date. Our captains monitor conditions closely and will communicate any concerns at least 12 hours before departure.

What is the minimum charter duration for a yacht in Dubai?

The minimum charter duration with Imperial Yachting is typically 2 hours for weekday bookings and 4 hours on weekends and public holidays.

Are food and drinks included in a Dubai yacht charter?

Standard charters include soft drinks, water, ice, and light snacks. Full catering packages can be arranged at an additional cost through our partner catering teams.
