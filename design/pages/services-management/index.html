Questions.

Common questions, answered as on the live page.

What yacht management services does Imperial Yachting offer?

Imperial Yachting provides comprehensive yacht management including charter management and revenue optimization, crew recruitment and training, maintenance scheduling and oversight, financial reporting and accounting, marketing and listing management, and berth management at Dubai Harbour.

How much revenue can my yacht generate through charter management?

It depends on the yacht's size and condition, on the season, and on how much of the calendar you keep for yourself. We don't publish a monthly average, the only figure worth anything to you is the one for your vessel. Send us the specification and the weeks you want to block, and we'll come back with a projection you can check line by line: charter rates for that size and season, our commission, and the running costs.

What percentage does Imperial Yachting charge for yacht management?

Imperial Yachting's management fee is structured as a performance-based commission on charter revenue, ensuring our interests are aligned with yours as the yacht owner. Contact us for a personalised management proposal.

What licences does Imperial Yachting hold for yacht management in Dubai?

Imperial Charter Yachting Services L.L.C. holds a Dubai trade licence (number 960574, company registration 2236727) and operates in full compliance with Dubai Maritime City Authority (DMCA) regulations. All managed vessels are fully insured and comply with UAE flag state requirements.
