Water
taxi.

Van Dutch is the tender you see chosen at superyachts, and you can spot it from the deck far off. We run one as a water taxi. Tell us where you are, and we come alongside your yacht, an island or a beach-club pier, take your party of up to 10 guests to Dubai Harbour, or to another marina you name.

If plans
change.

Cancel more than 48 hours before the start and you get everything back. Between 24 and 48 hours, 50%. Less than 24 hours, or if you do not show up, nothing, unless we re-sell your slot, in which case we refund you in full. If we cancel because of weather, safety or the boat, you choose between a full refund and a new date. Refunds go to the card you paid with within 2 business days; your bank may show them after 5 to 10.
